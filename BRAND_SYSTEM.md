# Access Home Care Ghana — Brand System v1

## Brand position
**Premium African Healthcare + Warm Human Care**

The visual identity should feel compassionate, trustworthy, calm and professionally managed. It should feel like a high-quality care organisation rather than a hospital, nursing-home template or generic corporate website.

## Core message
**Compassion. Dignity. Trust.**

Use this as the central emotional promise across the website. Supporting copy should reinforce personalised care, comfort, independence and family confidence.

### Usage rule
Burgundy is the primary action colour. Use it deliberately for CTAs, important links and key visual anchors. Avoid making every section burgundy. Large areas should remain white, cream or very light rose so the site feels calm and premium.

## Typography

Primary typeface: **Manrope**

- Headings: 700–800
- Body: 400–500
- Navigation / buttons: 700–800
- Eyebrows: 800, uppercase, wide tracking

Headings should be short, confident and human. Avoid excessive all-caps body copy.

## Shapes

- Buttons: pill-shaped
- Cards: 14–22px radius
- Images: 14–22px radius depending on context
- Avoid excessive rounded containers; use shape to create hierarchy, not decoration.

## Photography

Photography should show real human connection, dignity and everyday home environments. Prioritise Ghanaian/African representation, natural expressions, warm daylight and uncluttered compositions.

Avoid generic hospital imagery, overly staged stock photos and images that portray clients as helpless.

## Voice

The brand voice is:

- Warm, not sentimental
- Professional, not corporate
- Confident, not aggressive
- Reassuring, not vague
- Family-focused, not clinical

Prefer: **"Personalised care that helps your loved one live with comfort, dignity and confidence."**

Avoid: exaggerated medical claims, fear-based messaging, or promises that cannot be substantiated.

## CTA hierarchy

Primary: **Book a Care Consultation** / **Get Started**

Secondary: **Talk to Our Care Team** / **WhatsApp Us**

The website should make it easy for a Ghana-based family to call or start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Layout principles

1. Use generous white space.
2. Keep content widths controlled and readable.
3. Alternate white and warm-tint sections to create rhythm.
4. Use one strong focal message per section.
5. Let photography carry emotion; let typography carry clarity.
6. Use burgundy as an anchor rather than a blanket background.
